                         Technical Game Safety Guide
When you plug the console into a power outlet, always check the voltage label on the back
of the device. Make sure the cooling fans are free from dust so that air flows smoothly
through the system during long sessions.
Before you insert a new game disc into the drive, clean the surface gently with a soft cloth.
Dirt or fingerprints on the optical reader often cause reading errors in the middle of a
game.
Health experts recommend short rest periods for all players. Take a 10-minute break every
hour and step away from the screen to rest your eyes and stretch your muscle groups.
Players sometimes feel motion sickness or lightheadedness during fast-paced action
games. Discontinue playing immediately if you experience any discomfort, and consult a
medical doctor for advice.
